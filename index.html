Photoshop Auto Backup

This plugin automatically creates timestamped safety copies of the active Photoshop document (PSD/PSB) at a regular interval.

  • The original file is never modified, renamed or replaced.
  • Every backup is named with its date and time.
  • Old backups are pruned according to the retention limit.

To get started, choose the main backup folder. Photoshop asks for permission once: the grant is then remembered across sessions.
